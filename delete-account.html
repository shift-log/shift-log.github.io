Delete your ShiftLog account and data

ShiftLog by Md. Abid Raihan

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open ShiftLog on your phone or at shift-log.github.io.
  2. Go to Settings → Backup & sync.
  3. Tap Delete account and cloud data and confirm.

This permanently deletes, right away:

  • everything synced to your account: profile, work records, leave, notes and settings;
  • your ShiftLog sign-in account.

Data kept only on a device (if you never signed in, or after deleting the account) goes when you uninstall the app or clear the browser's site data. Backup files you exported are yours and are not affected.

By email

If you can't open the app, email abidcsedu@gmail.com from the Google account you used with ShiftLog, with the subject "Delete my ShiftLog account". Your account and all its data are deleted within 7 days, and you'll get a reply when it's done. Nothing is kept after deletion.
