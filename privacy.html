ShiftLog privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

ShiftLog helps you record your working hours, work-from-home days, leave and notes. This page explains what the app keeps, where, and how to remove it.

What ShiftLog stores

  • Your profile, as you enter it: name, gender, and, if you add them, date of birth, company, office ID, phone numbers, joining date and a profile photo.
  • Your work records: arrival and departure times, work mode (office, from home, outside office), where and why for outside-office work, an optional mood for the day, leave and holidays.
  • Your notes and tasks, and your app settings (look, reminders, button words).

Where it is kept

  • On your device, always. Without signing in, nothing leaves your device.
  • In your account, if you choose to sign in with Google: your records are synced to Google Cloud Firestore (Firebase, Mumbai region) so they appear on your other devices. Only your own account can read or write them.
  • When you sign in, Google tells ShiftLog your email address, name and account ID. They are used only to sign you in and keep your data under your account.

What ShiftLog does not do

  • No ads, no analytics or tracking, no selling or sharing of your data with anyone.
  • No location, contacts, camera roll or microphone access in the background. The camera and photos are used only when you pick a profile photo.
  • Reminders are scheduled on your phone; they are not sent through a server.

Backups you export

You can save a backup file or a spreadsheet. These go wherever you choose (Files, Drive, email). ShiftLog does not keep a copy.

Deleting your data

See how to delete your account and data. In short: Settings → Backup & sync → Delete account and cloud data removes everything in your account at once; uninstalling the app (or clearing the site's data in a browser) removes what is on that device.

Children

ShiftLog is a work tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will show the new date at the top.
